Research & Selected Projects

Ongoing Research • 2026–Present Sharif University of Technology

Motion Perception Capabilities in Vision-Language Models

Advisor: Dr. Mahdieh Soleymani Baghshah (Dept. of Computer Engineering)

While state-of-the-art Vision-Language Models excel at static object recognition, they frequently struggle with fine-grained temporal dynamics, trajectory understanding, and directional motion grounding. This research systematically diagnoses spatiotemporal failure modes in modern VLMs and explores representation-level and prompting interventions to improve motion-aware reasoning.

Vision-Language Models Temporal Grounding PyTorch Multimodal Evaluation

Compositionality in Vision-Language Models: Benchmarks, Bottlenecks & System 2 Remedies

Conducted a comprehensive literature survey investigating why contrastive and generative VLMs often behave like “bags of words” when binding attributes, relations, and spatial configurations. Synthesized recent advances in hard-negative contrastive fine-tuning, neuro-symbolic decomposition, and chain-of-thought visual reasoning.

Compositional Reasoning System 2 Thinking Contrastive Learning VLMs
